About us

Retro, loud and friendly.

Piu Piu Piu sits inside The Zhongshan Building, a creative hub on Jalan Rotan in Kampung Attap. The bar is small, with lucky cats on the shelves, rock and roll on the speakers and Japanese retro details everywhere.

Drop in between browsing the building's shops, sit outside with an iced coffee, and take a look at the t-shirt merch designed by a Japanese tattoo artist.
