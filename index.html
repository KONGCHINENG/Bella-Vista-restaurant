Cooking the way our nonna did

Bella Vista started as a six-table room above a wine shop. Everything on the menu is still made the same way it was that first year — pasta rolled by hand each morning, a sauce that simmers for most of the day, and bread that never sees a freezer.

We take walk-ins when we can, but the room is small and fills up fast. A reservation is the only way to guarantee your table.
